Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Personal Drive Sync is used only by its owner to sync files between the owner's own Google Drive and the owner's own computer.

  • Access to Google Drive is used solely to download and upload the owner's own files.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold, or shared with anyone.
  • Files move only directly between Google Drive and the owner's computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
